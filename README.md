## Generate from an idea

Describe an image and generate it right from the homepage. Choose a model, aspect ratio, and image count; add references, mention an image in the prompt, or use `{slots}` to make several variations at once.

<p align="center"><a href="https://muvloom.online/"><img src="./docs/images/home-en.webp" alt="Centered Muvloom homepage showing the Generate and Canvas switch and image prompt controls" width="1100" /></a></p>

## Refine on an infinite canvas

Arrange images, select one or several as references, and generate new versions beside the originals. Draw, add arrows or text to explain a change, and keep editing while jobs run. In deployments that enable the AI assistant, you can also discuss an idea beside the canvas and confirm its proposed image or video generation before it runs.

## Explore and reuse

Find examples in the inspiration library, then adapt a prompt or reference for your own work. Your works can be searched, favorited, downloaded, or sent back to the canvas; save useful images as assets and prompts with their references and settings as templates.

<p align="center"><img src="./docs/images/inspiration-library.webp" alt="Muvloom Explore view with public inspiration examples and category filters" width="1100" /></p>

Switch between Chinese and English, light and dark appearance, and desktop or mobile layouts. Where account sync is enabled, projects and saved creative assets can follow you across devices.
